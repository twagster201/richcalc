Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is RichCalc?

RichCalc is a multi-calculator web app offering both Health and Financial calculators in a single, user-friendly interface.

2. Which calculators are available?

RichCalc provides BMI, BMR, and Body Fat calculators for health, along with Mortgage, Compound Interest, Payment, and other financial calculators.

3. Does RichCalc store my personal data?

No. RichCalc does not collect or store personal information, nor does it use cookies or tracking technologies.

4. How accurate are the calculators?

All calculators use well-known formulas or estimation methods. However, they are for informational purposes only. Always consult qualified professionals for personalized advice.

5. Can I use RichCalc on mobile devices?

Yes! The site is built with a responsive design, so you can use RichCalc on smartphones, tablets, or desktop computers.
